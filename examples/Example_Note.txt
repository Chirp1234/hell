Welcome to NOTEVIEW!

Controls in a note:
- up/down: scroll one line
- left/right: previous/next page
- enter: next page
- clear: back to the note list

Controls in the note list:
- up/down: choose a note
- enter or right: open it
- clear: quit

Make your own notes as .txt or .md files. Each file becomes one note, named after the file. Long lines wrap automatically to fit the 31-character screen.
